Alias URL
The URL you create, without the blog URL. An alias "aboutme" is caught when someone opens http://yourblog/aboutme.
Alias destination
The resource served by the alias, without the blog URL. To serve the static page "aboutme", the destination is "pages/aboutme".
Visible redirection
If checked, visitors are redirected to the destination and see its URL. Otherwise the alias URL stays in the address bar.
